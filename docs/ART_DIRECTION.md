# Sally reference and production notes

## Visual source

The user supplied `Sally the Sexy Salmon Character Sheet.png` in this chat on 2026-10-28. The sheet, not the earlier rectangular Suki blockout, defines the character. No local copy of that original attachment was available to add to this checkout; the user's original file remains unchanged.

The image's slogans and usage examples are reference content, not instructions to create promotions or post on social networks. No social-media account publishing is performed by this kit.

## Identity to preserve

- Round chibi head, about two-thirds of the standing character's height.
- Layered glossy salmon slices forming a bob with swept bangs and cream marbling.
- Distinct warm peach face, large expressive green eyes with black upper lashes, cheek blush and a smile with teeth/tongue.
- A broad avocado slice running perpendicular to the fish, tapering down only to the forehead.
- Green jalapeño ring, cream seeds and a single red hot-sauce dot.
- Lower body/collar built from visible individual white rice grains.
- Slim glossy black nori limbs, gloves with fingers and chunky cuffed boots.
- Signature peace-sign pose. No lower-body green band.
- Happy, wink, flirty, excited, sassy and surprised expressions.

## Deliverable boundaries

The glossy transparent hero and six-cell expression atlas are AI-assisted illustrated assets based on the reference identity. Their apparent 3D shading does not mean a corresponding mesh was reconstructed from the image. They are supplied as raster PNGs, with no claim of vector paths or facial rigging.

The independently authored SVG and Blender generators interpret those features in editable geometry. They are simplified versions with controlled shapes and portable materials. The actual mesh renders are under `assets/3d/` and are displayed separately from illustrated artwork in the gallery. Expression files are distinct models, not a continuously deformable facial rig.

## Raster artwork art direction

Hero: transparent full-body three-quarter Sally, glossy salmon-slice curls and marbling, peach face, green eyes/lashes, avocado fringe, seeded jalapeño with one sauce dot, rice collar, peace-sign glove and shiny black boots; no text, logos or background.

Atlas: same character identity in three equal columns by two equal rows, transparent background, complete bodies centered within cells. Order: happy / wink / flirty across the top; excited / sassy / surprised across the bottom. No labels or props. The gallery uses the image's natural dimensions to export each cell without resampling.

The editable generator output can be regenerated with Python/Blender from VS Code. The glossy illustrations are static art inputs and cannot be regenerated by the procedural scripts.
